      ## Our approach 

      Is to remove unnecessary complexity and overheads from projects. There are definitely scenarios where we will reach for the tools to manage things such as real-time reactivity and simultaneous, high-traffic connections to your site. But 90% of websites don't need to be encumbered with functionality that will never be needed.


      So, we assess your requirements and then strip back as much of the technical apparatus as possible. If something isn't needed, *it goes*. If it might be needed later on, *it still goes*, but the modular nature of our builds ensures extra functionality can slotted into place in the future.


      The codebase is kept minimal, website assets such as images are optimised and you'll learn about the tools you can use to keep media file sizes to a minimum once the project is handed over.


      This Theme is dedicated to advancing the science of mental health and
      wellbeing through the development of innovative, nature-based
      interventions that promote both human and planetary flourishing.
      Termed Ecological Collective Flourishing (E-Co-Flourishing), the approach
      integrates physical, emotional, social, and ecological dimensions of
      wellbeing. The research aims to understand and harness the mechanisms by
      which engagement with green spaces and cultural institutions can support
      mental health, particularly in non-clinical settings. By developing and
      testing scalable, evidence-based programmes, the Theme seeks to contribute
      to a new model of preventative mental healthcare rooted in community and
      environmental engagement.
